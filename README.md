### Aakash Bansal

Quantitative modelling for credit, mortgages and derivatives. MS in Financial Engineering from the University of Illinois. Previously a research associate at Harvard Business School, built the HELOC prepayment model at Figure Lending, and worked in Home Lending credit risk at JPMorgan Chase.

**Selected work**

- [nvix-volatility-channels](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/nvix-volatility-channels): which kinds of front-page news predict stock market volatility, 1926 to 2016, tested only on windows the text model never saw.
- [spx-vix-neural-sde](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/spx-vix-neural-sde): joint SPX and VIX smile calibration with a neural SDE, with the bid-ask coverage metrics, dynamic loss weights and warm starts I built for a J.P. Morgan-sponsored practicum.
- [agency-mbs-prepayment](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/agency-mbs-prepayment): prepayment, credit and loss severity on Fannie Mae loan performance data. When rates dipped in September 2024, the 2023 vintage's largest loans went from 7.6% CPR in July to 16.0% in October.
- [heston-calibration](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/heston-calibration): a Heston pricer checked five independent ways and calibrated to 1,434 live SPX quotes.
- [ml-in-finance](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/ml-in-finance): walk-forward VIX forecasts, where a three-term HAR regression beats a random forest and a neural network.
- [cpp-option-pricing](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/cpp-option-pricing): barrier Monte Carlo in C++ with a Brownian-bridge correction, variance reduction and trinomial trees.

Also: [pde-option-pricer](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/pde-option-pricer), [lp-finance](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/lp-finance), [time-series-econometrics](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/time-series-econometrics), [cornell-case-competition](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/cornell-case-competition), [sector-signal-backtest](https://github.com/aakashbansalgit/sector-signal-backtest).
